ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Snehand

I'm a photographer and cinematographer based in Kerala. To me, taking photos is about freezing a feeling. The best moments are the real ones—the quick glances, the happy tears, and the genuine laughter. My goal is to capture those honest emotions and turn them into memories you can keep forever. Whether it's a wedding, a portrait, or a film, I just want to tell your story beautifully. When I'm not working, I'm usually chasing the monsoon clouds or drinking too much chai. I'd love to hear your story and help you tell it.
